# Character appearance references

Research date: 14 October 2026. These references informed the original, stylised geometry and portraits in the game. All nine people's labelled photographs were visually inspected in a browser. Four photographs were subsequently downloaded temporarily as appearance references for AI-generated playable-avatar portraits. The resulting original illustrations are bundled with the game; the source photographs are not bundled, fetched at runtime, or used as gameplay model textures.

The names below are the user's requested fictional parody names. The arcade roles, chase mechanics, dialogue, and abilities are fictional. Inclusion in this roster does not assert that any person is a member of the Cockroach Janta Party or that the events depicted occurred.

Abhiteep Bauna, German Sepherd, Pappu, and Khujliwaal are the four playable caricature avatars. Udhar Galib and Jaleel Imaan are reward-only characters retained for the fictional victory meme; they are excluded from avatar selection. Mobi ji, Amit kaka, and Baba ji are pursuers. The table retains appearance references for all of these roles.

### Generated portrait assets

The four current playable-avatar selection portraits are deliberately exaggerated 2D cartoons with thick outlines, simple facial shapes, and oversized heads. Each is a 640 × 640 WebP file; together they occupy 106.37 kB (about 104 KiB). They were restyled with built-in `image_gen` from the earlier generated portraits, retaining broad hair, beard, glasses, and outfit cues while explicitly discarding exact facial proportions and photographic detail. [AVATAR_CARTOON_PROMPTS.json](AVATAR_CARTOON_PROMPTS.json) records the current v3 prompts, input assets, and outputs. [AVATAR_ART_PROMPTS.json](AVATAR_ART_PROMPTS.json) preserves the superseded v2 prompts and original reference URLs.

- `src/assets/avatars/abhiteep-v3.webp`
- `src/assets/avatars/german-v3.webp`
- `src/assets/avatars/pappu-v3.webp`
- `src/assets/avatars/khujliwaal-v3.webp`

The v2 files remain as local source history and are excluded from the production build. Gameplay characters remain animated 3D models built in code. The pursuer and reward portraits remain SVG artwork. No generation service or remote reference-image request is needed during gameplay. This visual restyle is not a legal clearance or a guarantee against claims.

## RSS-inspired arcade squad clothing

The [NDTV photograph accompanying its uniform-change report](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/rss-says-goodbye-to-iconic-khaki-shorts-switches-to-trousers-1472678) was visually inspected: [direct image](https://i.ndtvimg.com/i/2016-10/rss-trousers-full-pants_650x400_41476152954.jpg?downsize=400%3A225).

Visible clothing cues are white tucked button-up shirts, often with rolled sleeves, brown full-length trousers, dark belts, shallow black caps, and black shoes. The original game squad uses these simplified clothing colours and silhouettes on generic adult characters. It does not reproduce insignia, photographed people, poses, or carried objects.

The game uses plain unlettered cloth, varied casual tops, and generic adult original models. It does not reproduce emblems, slogans, photographed people, or weapons.

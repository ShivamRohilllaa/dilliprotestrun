THE GAME & HOW TO PLAY

Play Dilli Protest Run: a free Delhi runner game

Dilli Protest Run is a fictional Delhi-inspired arcade runner. Choose a cartoon avatar, a location, an issue and a pursuer, then lead your crowd through a three-lane chase. Its characters, slogans and endings are satire, with no affiliation or endorsement.

Make it to 1,000 metres

Move between lanes to avoid obstacles, jump over low barriers and slide under banners. Collect chai tokens along the way. Finish the run to earn 100 bonus tokens and reveal a fictional victory meme. Reaching 80 collected tokens brings extra pursuers into the chase.

  • Keyboard: use the arrow keys or A/D to move, W or Space to jump, and S to slide.
  • Touch: swipe on the game or use its arrow buttons.
  • Press P or Escape to pause. Settings include sound, reduced motion and graphics quality.

Your progress stays in this browser

Chai tokens unlock locations; they have no cash value. Your best score, choices and settings are saved locally. There is no player account, and clearing browser data removes this progress. Once loaded, the game can keep running without a network connection.

Play on mobile or desktop

No download or account is needed. Choose Jantar Mantar, India Gate or Ramlila Maidan as your route, with more locations unlocked through play. The game runs in your browser and needs JavaScript and WebGL 2 graphics support.

Having trouble starting the game?

Use an updated browser with graphics acceleration enabled. In desktop Chrome, find “Use graphics acceleration when available” under Settings → System and relaunch the browser after turning it on. On mobile, open the site directly in Chrome or Safari. You can also try low graphics to reduce rendering load.
